Activities

Learning, growth, and community at the heart of Aldershot

Weekday Madrasah (Ages 7 to 14)

Our weekday Madrasah provides a nurturing and structured environment for children aged 7 to 14. We focus on building a strong Islamic foundation through:

Qur'an Recitation and Tajwid

Children learn to recite the Qur'an with correct pronunciation and fluency. We gradually introduce the rules of Tajwid so they can recite confidently and beautifully.

Islamic Studies

Students learn the essentials of their faith, including beliefs, worship, manners, Seerah, and daily duas in an engaging and age-appropriate manner. We aim to nurture character, discipline, and love for Allah and His Messenger ﷺ.

Qur'anic Arabic

Basic Qur'anic Arabic is taught to help students understand the Qur'an directly. Children learn key vocabulary, simple grammar, and the structure of Qur'anic sentences in a fun and interactive way.

Our Madrasah prioritises akhlaq, respect, and a supportive learning atmosphere, ensuring every child feels encouraged and valued.

Qur'an Circle After Isha

Every evening after Isha, brothers gather for our Qur'an Circle. It is a warm and welcoming space dedicated to improving recitation and understanding.

What We Do:

  • Group recitation of the Qur'an
  • One-to-one correction of pronunciation and Tajwid
  • Teaching of basic Tajwid rules in a practical, easy-to-understand way
  • Short discussions on Tafsir and reflections from classical and contemporary scholars

It is a spiritually uplifting circle that strengthens brotherhood, increases confidence in reading, and helps us connect deeply with the words of Allah. All brothers are welcome, regardless of their level, beginners to advanced.

Community Children's Events

Every few months, we host special events for children and families. These gatherings include:

  • Talks by visiting scholars and teachers
  • Engaging reminders and stories tailored for young hearts
  • Interactive sessions for children and adults
  • Refreshments and social time for families

These events bring the community together, inspire our children, and create a warm environment for learning and connection. Everyone, including parents, children, and community members, is welcome and encouraged to attend.

A welcoming home for Muslims of all backgrounds in Aldershot, offering a space for prayer, reflection, and connection.
