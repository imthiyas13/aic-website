Madrasah

Nurturing faith, knowledge, and character in our children

Our weekday Madrasah offers structured Islamic education for children aged 7–14, focusing on Qur'an recitation with Tajwīd, Islamic Studies, and Qur'anic Arabic. We aim to nurture not only knowledge, but also good manners, confidence, and a strong Islamic identity.

Children learn the fundamentals of their faith in a positive and engaging environment, guided by teachers who care about their spiritual and moral development. The Madrasah also helps parents by building consistent habits of learning, discipline, and love for the deen.

Through this programme, we hope to raise a generation that is connected to the Qur'an, grounded in Islamic values, and proud of their faith.
