Make your donation worth 25% more

جَزَاكَ ٱللَّٰهُ خَيْرًا

If you pay UK tax, the masjid can claim an extra 25p from HMRC for every £1 you give — at no cost to you. Scan the code and fill in the short form once.

Scan to add Gift Aid

It takes about a minute

  1. 1Scan the code with your phone camera.
  2. 2Enter your name and home address. HMRC needs these to approve the claim.
  3. 3Tick the declaration and submit. That's it — you only ever need to do this once.

Your declaration also covers donations you've already made in the past 4 years and any you make in future, so a £20 donation becomes £25 to the masjid.

Who can Gift Aid?

You can Gift Aid if you pay UK Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax, and the tax you pay in the year is at least the amount all the charities you support will claim on your donations. It costs you nothing extra.

Not a UK taxpayer? Please skip this form — your donation is no less valued.
