Charity & Donation

Supporting the house of Allah through your generosity

Charity is at the heart of our community, and your donations play a vital role in keeping the masjid running smoothly throughout the year. From covering essential expenses such as rent, utilities, maintenance, and cleaning, to supporting our educational programmes and community services, every contribution helps us serve you better.

The masjid relies entirely on the generosity of worshippers, and your support ensures that this house of Allah remains open, active, and welcoming for everyone—whether they come daily or only for Jumu'ah. By giving for the sake of Allah, you help build a stronger, more connected Muslim community in Aldershot.
