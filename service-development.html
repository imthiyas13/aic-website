Mosque Development

Building a permanent home for the Muslims of Aldershot

At the moment, our masjid operates from a rented building with a high monthly cost, which places a significant financial burden on our community. Our long-term vision is to purchase a permanent building that will serve as a stable, secure, and spacious home for the Muslims of Aldershot.

Owning our masjid will allow us to expand our services, create better facilities for brothers, sisters, and children, and build a centre that future generations can proudly call their own. We are inviting the community to join us in this noble and historic effort—every donation, no matter the amount, brings us one step closer to establishing a permanent house of Allah in our town.
