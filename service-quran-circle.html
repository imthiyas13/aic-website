Qur'an Circle

Reconnecting with the Book of Allah every night after 'Isha

Our Qur'an Circle takes place every night after 'Isha and provides a peaceful, supportive environment for brothers to reconnect with the Book of Allah. We recite together, correct pronunciation and Tajwīd, and learn the rules in a simple and practical way.

From time to time, we also reflect on the meanings of verses and read short passages of tafsir to deepen our understanding. The circle welcomes all levels—from beginners to those who recite fluently—and creates a beautiful sense of brotherhood and spiritual growth.

It is a chance to improve one's recitation, strengthen one's heart, and keep a regular connection with the Qur'an throughout the week.
